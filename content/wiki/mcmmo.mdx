# MCMMO

MCMMO adds RPG-style skills to Minecraft — players level individual skills by performing actions, unlocking abilities and passive bonuses.

## Common Skills

- **Mining** — level by mining ores; higher levels increase double drops and speed bonuses.
- **Woodcutting** — level by chopping wood; unlock abilities like Tree Feller.
- **Excavation** — leveling by digging dirt, gravel, sand.

## Abilities

- Abilities are triggered with specific tools or key combos (e.g., bash, activation items). Check the in-game help or /mcmmo commands for exact controls.

## Tips

- Group up with friends to share resource gathering and experience.
- Use ability cooldowns wisely for efficiency.

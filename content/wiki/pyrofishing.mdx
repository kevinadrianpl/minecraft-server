# Pyrofishing

Pyrofishing is a custom activity where players use special bait and gear to fish up flame-themed loot. It mixes typical fishing mechanics with server-specific drops.

## How to Pyrofishing

- Obtain a Pyro Bait (server store or event reward).
- Use the bait with a fishing rod at designated hot springs or lava-coast locations.
- Successful catches can yield rare items or currency.

## Loot Examples

- Emberfish (consumable)
- Pyro Scales (trading material)
- Chance for rare cosmetics or crates

## Strategy

- Fish during server events for increased drop rates.
- Use higher-tier rods to improve your chances.

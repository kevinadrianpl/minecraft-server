# Towny

Towny is a land-ownership and nation system that allows players to form towns, claim land, and protect builds. Below are the basics to get started.

## Creating a Town

- Use `/towny new <townname>` to create a town (cost may vary depending on server configs).
- Claim land with `/towny claim` in the area you want to protect.

## Town Management

- Invite players with `/towny invite <player>` and promote them using `/towny rank <player>`.
- Manage town settings, taxes, and plots via town commands in-game.

## Tips

- Coordinate with your town to avoid overlapping claims.
- Build near farmable areas and resources for easier economy growth.

# Pyromining

Pyromining focuses on mining in volcanic areas where special ores spawn. These ores are used in crafting flame-themed equipment and are valuable on the server economy.

## Locations

- Volcanic belts and nether-frontier zones commonly house pyromineral veins.
- Look for dark rock strata with red/orange flecks in nicely marked zones.

## Equipment

- Use reinforced pickaxes to survive hazardous terrain.
- Bring fire resistance potions or armor with fire protection.

## Rewards

- Pyro Ore: smelts into Pyro Ingot for unique gear.
- Rare drop chance: Flux Shard (used for high-end recipes).
